## Honest notes before the demo

- **SIA revenue share counts recorded commercial progression only.** A Senior Industry Advisor counts with a recorded placement, offer or commercial progression. The seed carries one such case: David Okello introduced Civic Services Partnership (lead source "SIA introduction"), the advisory opportunity closed won at £42k, and the engagement ran as a closed 20% placement. Everything else about SIAs on screen is at interview stage and says so. Data version is 23; an older browser store reseeds itself on load. Nothing on screen says "demo", "showcase" or "fictional"; figures not read from a record are labelled "estimate".
- **Offerings are still tags underneath.** The Offerings page reads the recorded values on associates, accounts and deals and groups near-matches ("Advisory" with "Senior Industry Advisors"). A governed taxonomy would sharpen it; the mechanism is there.
- **Inferences are labelled.** Scarcity premiums, founder-dependency trends, second-degree reach and the CV parser are inferences or showcase figures and say so on screen. Facts carry the green tick; inferences the indigo sparkle.
- **Ordering is deliberate.** Home ranks recorded business risks (an account resting on one person, an uncovered proposal) above routine overdue tasks; the Sales spotlight leads with deals and recorded relationships, and estimated network signals support from the rail. The Offerings detail rail ends in "The decision": network experience, bench gap inside 90 days and the open briefs, on one screen, so her public-sector question is answered in one place.
- **Automations never act alone.** Every rule proposes on Home and waits for Approve; Settings → Automations switches each rule off.
- **No live connectors.** LinkedIn, Salesforce and SharePoint are shown as demo or future. "Prepared search" opens a web query; it does not trawl LinkedIn. Say this before she asks.
- **Sample versus network.** Charts use the 511 seeded profiles as the denominator and say so. The 524 network figure is a headline, never a chart base. The generated profiles are tagged "demo-sample · fictional" on the record.
- **Nothing is sent.** Quotes, emails and WhatsApp are logged, not delivered.
