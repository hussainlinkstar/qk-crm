Product scope & delivery brief

Qiko CRM

One clear home for every prospect, every meaningful interaction, and the next action the team should take.

Qiko CRM is the sales workspace inside Qiko Core. It brings Signal registrations, website enquiries, completed scans, and manually added prospects into a single, traceable customer record—then helps the team act on the right follow-up.

One person. One record. Shared context from first signal to next step.

At a glance

Product intent and delivery boundaries for the initial release.

Primary user

Sales team

Starting with the personalised workspace for Ed and the sales team.

MVP focus

Sales workspace

Home, Sales overview, unified leads, lead profile, pipeline, and actions.

Product principle

Actions over vanity

Metrics give context; evidence-backed follow-up actions drive the workflow.

Product outcome

Help the team understand who a prospect is, what has happened, what is confirmed, and what to do next—without losing source attribution or inventing customer facts.

Scope boundary

What the first release includes—and what it deliberately does not claim.

In the V1 product experience

  • Sales-first workspace, lead records, profiles, pipeline, tasks, and actions.
  • Sample/demo records for product walkthrough and browser-local edits.
  • Grounded summaries and suggestions based on data available in the workspace.

Not a live data promise

  • Production Signal, website, or Admin data feeds are not live in the current demo.
  • Marketing, Finance Ops, and Qiko Pulse are future scope.
  • Demo sign-in does not verify a real account or password.

02 · PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Functional scope

A focused workspace for the full sales day

The CRM experience is organised around a simple loop: prioritise, inspect, act, and keep the record current.

01 · Home

Start with today's priorities

  • Personal greeting and Ask Qiko entry point.
  • Up to three high- or medium-priority groups, with people and tasks to review.
  • Pending, in-progress, and completed task snapshot.
  • Recent activity and business changes, such as new leads, recent scans, wins, and overdue tasks.
  • Sales is available; future workspaces are labelled “Soon”.

02 · Sales overview

See supported metrics, then take action

  • Total leads, Signal sign-ups, completed free scans, active users (30 days), new enquiries, and follow-ups due.
  • Daily sign-up and completed-scan trends for 7, 14, or 30 days when valid history exists.
  • Pipeline counts across New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, and Lost.
  • Lead sources, engaged open leads, and suggested actions with evidence.
  • Add a lead or ask Qiko for a sales summary.

03 · Unified leads

Find and manage the right record

  • Search by name, company, or email; filter by status, source, owner, or activity.
  • Activity filters for attention, scan, recent use, open task, or unassigned owner.
  • Sort by last activity, newest, engagement, or name; export filtered results to CSV.
  • Paginated list (15 per page); row opens quick panel, name opens full profile.
  • Confirm status and owner changes; block duplicate email on edit.

04 · Pipeline

Make progress visible

  • Five stages: New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, and Lost.
  • Cards show company, source, owner, last activity, and engagement.
  • Search and filters are consistent with the lead list.
  • Drag to another stage with confirmation before the record changes.
  • Each lead's next action is informed by a note, open task, or Qiko suggestion.

One complete lead profile

Customer context should remain attached to the record, not scattered across screens.

Identity & engagement

Name, role, company, email, phone, source, status, owner, next action, and engagement score out of 100.

Record tabs

Overview, Activity, Conversations, Scans and reports, Tasks, and Notes.

Activity timeline

Product, scan, enquiry, and CRM activity with source and timestamp when available.

Conversations & follow-up

Log calls, email, WhatsApp, meetings, or messages; compose messages; create tasks from a lead or scan.

03 · WORKFLOWS & DATA

Work to be done

Make every recommendation explainable

An action is a piece of work with an owner, a reason, and a clear outcome—not just a metric or an alert.

Action workspace

Turn follow-up into trackable work

  • Suggested actions can be accepted, edited, or dismissed; accepting creates a task.
  • Task board states: Pending, In progress, Completed; also filter Overdue and Dismissed.
  • Board/list views, search, and filters by owner or priority.
  • Each task retains due date, owner, linked lead, evidence, and change history.
  • Drag to update task state; preserve list and board context.

Suggestion rules

Suggest only when there's a reason

  • New Signal sign-up not yet contacted.
  • Scan completed within the last 72 hours.
  • Website enquiry waiting for a reply.
  • Strong product use in the last three days.
  • Contacted or qualified lead with no follow-up for seven days.
  • Do not show a suggestion if the lead is closed or already has an open task.

Ask Qiko

Useful answers remain grounded in records the user can inspect.

Ask in plain language

Today’s sales, recent Signal sign-ups, completed scans, who to follow up with, pending tasks, pipeline, or one prospect.

Show the evidence

Each response separates the answer, relevant facts and source, and the recommended next step.

Confirm before changes

Supported actions can create a task, update a status, or open a page; consequential changes require confirmation.

04 · DELIVERY & ACCEPTANCE

Release plan

Ship the useful sales loop first

Keep the MVP modular: prove the daily workflow with clearly labelled demo data, then connect validated sources without changing the user-facing record model.

P0 · Core MVP

Reliable daily sales operations

  • Authentication experience and personalised Home.
  • Sales dashboard and supported KPI presentation.
  • Unified lead search, filtering, profile, and activity history.
  • Tasks, notes, lead actions, and pipeline stage management.
  • Clear demo labeling and browser-local demo reset.

P1 · Intelligence

Evidence-led assistance

  • Ask Qiko grounded in available CRM records.
  • Follow-up recommendations with facts and reasoning.
  • Time-based activity trends only where history is valid.
  • Confirmed execution for supported changes.

Acceptance checklist

V1 is ready when a sales user can complete these outcomes without misleading data.

  • Sign in and reach a useful Home workspace.
  • Review priorities and open related leads or tasks.
  • View available Sales KPIs with accurate labels.
  • Search and filter the lead list; open a lead profile.
  • Understand a prospect's source and activity timeline.
  • Create, assign, update, and complete tasks.
  • Update pipeline status with confirmation.
  • Review an AI suggestion's evidence before accepting it.
  • Ask a grounded question and inspect its facts.
  • Identify sample data and reset it without confusion.

Explicitly later / out of current demo

Live integrations

Production synchronisation with Qiko Signal, website, and Admin services until APIs, permissions, and field coverage are validated.

Future workspaces

Marketing campaigns, Finance Ops, and Qiko Pulse remain visible as future directions, not usable V1 features.

Production identity

Real account verification and password recovery are outside the current demo sign-in behavior.

Delivery guardrail: Do not present an unavailable integration as live. Show source, freshness, and errors clearly; keep the CRM data contract stable while providers evolve.

Product direction

Start with Sales. Preserve a clear route to future workspaces and Qiko Pulse, but let evidence, reliable records, and completed follow-ups—not future-facing UI—define the first release.
